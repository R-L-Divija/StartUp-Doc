Your Startup Journey

1. Company Registration Certificate

  • Choose business structure → Private Limited, LLP, OPC, Partnership.
  • Reserve company name via RUN on MCA portal.
  • Prepare ID & address proof of directors/partners.
  • Provide office address proof.
  • Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for directors.
  • Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN).
  • File incorporation form (SPICe+) with MoA, AoA.
  • MCA verification & approval → Get Certificate of Incorporation with CIN.

2. Tax Registration Certificate (GST in India)

  • Check GST applicability based on turnover or interstate supply.
  • Collect PAN, business address proof, and bank details.
  • Apply for GST online using DSC.
  • GST officer verification (may include physical visit).
  • Receive GSTIN & Registration Certificate.

3. Government License Certificate

  • Identify required licenses (FSSAI, Shops & Establishment, Trade License, etc.).
  • Collect business proof, owner ID proof, NOC if rented.
  • Apply via local/state department portal.
  • Pay applicable fees.
  • Inspection (if required) and receive license certificate.
